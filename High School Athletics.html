Teams for boys and girls

We welcome students, families, and supporters to our athletics program. Our teams give young athletes of every experience level the chance to develop their skills, build lasting friendships, and represent our community with pride.

Participation fosters teamwork, discipline, perseverance, and sportsmanship. Whether a student is new to organized sports or a seasoned competitor, there is a place for them here.

Our commitment

  • Equal opportunityBoys and girls programs receive the same dedication, quality coaching, and support.
  • InclusionEvery student is encouraged to participate, regardless of background or ability.
  • SportsmanshipWe expect respect toward teammates, opponents, officials, and spectators.
  • Student well-beingAcademic success, health, and safety always come first.

Fall season

Soccer

Boys Soccer: A fast-paced, skill-focused program emphasizing teamwork, fitness, and tactical understanding.

Girls Soccer: A competitive and supportive program that develops technical ability and confidence on the field.

Cross Country

Boys Cross Country: Athletes build endurance and mental toughness while competing in invitationals and championship meets.

Girls Cross Country: Runners of all paces train together, setting personal goals and celebrating individual progress.

Volleyball

Girls Volleyball: A spirited program focused on communication, quick reflexes, and cooperative play.

Boys Volleyball: An emerging program that introduces athletes to the fundamentals of serving, setting, and attacking.

Field Hockey and Football

Girls Field Hockey: Athletes learn stick skills, strategy, and the value of precise teamwork.

Boys Football: A structured program that teaches physical conditioning, strategy, and leadership, with a strong emphasis on safety.

Winter season

Basketball

Boys Basketball: Players refine their shooting, defense, and court awareness through disciplined practice and competitive games.

Girls Basketball: A dynamic program that builds fundamentals, confidence, and a strong team identity.

Swimming and Diving

Boys Swimming and Diving: Swimmers and divers train for individual and relay events in a focused, encouraging environment.

Girls Swimming and Diving: Athletes pursue personal bests while supporting one another at every meet.

Wrestling and Gymnastics

Boys Wrestling: A program that emphasizes technique, discipline, and respect for one's opponent.

Girls Gymnastics: Athletes develop strength, flexibility, and poise across vault, bars, beam, and floor.

Ice Hockey

Boys Ice Hockey: Players develop skating, puck handling, and team strategy.

Girls Ice Hockey: An inclusive and competitive program for athletes who love the speed and teamwork of the game.

Spring season

Baseball and Softball

Boys Baseball: Players grow in hitting, fielding, pitching, and game awareness.

Girls Softball: A welcoming program that teaches fundamentals while fostering strong team bonds.

Track and Field

Boys Track and Field: Athletes compete in sprints, distance events, relays, jumps, and throws.

Girls Track and Field: Athletes pursue excellence in a wide range of events, with coaching tailored to every skill level.

Lacrosse

Boys Lacrosse: A fast, physical sport that rewards agility, coordination, and teamwork.

Girls Lacrosse: An energetic program that emphasizes stick skills, strategy, and sportsmanship.

Tennis and Golf

Boys Tennis: Players develop technique and match strategy in singles and doubles competition.

Girls Tennis: Athletes build skill, focus, and resilience through regular practice and team matches.

Boys and Girls Golf: Open to all students, with instruction in the fundamentals of the game and course etiquette.

How to join

  1. Review the scheduleIdentify the sports and seasons that interest you.
  2. Get a physicalA current physical from a licensed health provider is required.
  3. Submit formsParent or guardian consent and emergency contact information must be on file.
  4. Attend a meeting or tryoutCoaches will share expectations, schedules, and evaluation details.
  5. Meet eligibilityStudents are expected to remain in good academic standing.

Expectations for athletes and families

  • Attend practices and games consistently and arrive on time.
  • Communicate respectfully with coaches, teammates, and officials.
  • Maintain academic responsibilities alongside athletic commitments.
  • Support all teams, boys and girls alike, with enthusiasm and good conduct.
